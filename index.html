Unity & C# · FMODMixing Learning Mobile Game

Mix Master — Sonical.ly

Mix Master is an educational game for Android & iOS that teaches audio-mixing concepts through an interactive simulation of a mixing console, multiple tracks, and effect controls. The experience also simulates plugin-style interactions inspired by a DAW, within the capabilities of the FMOD integration. My role began in technical sound design and expanded into gameplay programming, UI, shaders, and other systems.

3 Game Modes

Training, Mutiplayer and Mix Maker. Users can learn audio and mixing basics, compete versus other users, and build their own mix in a basic DAW simulator.

6 Main Effects

Gain, Pan, Reverb, Delay, EQ, and Compression; presented through an interactive mixing-console simulation. These effects have 4 tier levels and derived plug-ins.

UI & Shaders
